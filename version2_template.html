A population-based resource for the prioritization of rare variants of unknown significance in clinical genomics

Sarah Tremblay, Claudia Moreau, Gilles-Philippe Morin, Mylène Gagnon, Catherine Laprise and Simon Girard

Abstract. Using whole-genome sequencing in the Saguenay-Lac-Saint-Jean founder population, founder variants of interest were identified through population enrichment and IBD sharing. Most would likely have been missed by conventional clinically driven approaches, highlighting the value of population structure-based strategies for rare disease variant discovery.
